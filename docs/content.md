# 《雅思阅读的领域语言》最终版目录与写作规范

副标题：在雅思主题范围内学习世界如何被英语表达
English Title: **Domain Language for IELTS Reading**

---

# 1. Book Positioning（全书定位）

This book is a domain-language reader for IELTS Reading（雅思阅读领域语言读本）.

It helps readers enter the common topic worlds of IELTS Reading: science, environment, life, climate, education, psychology, health, economy, work, cities, agriculture, technology, history, media, design, and culture.

Each chapter introduces one domain world（领域世界）. The writing explains how that domain works, what problems it studies, how different factors interact, and how English expresses those facts, mechanisms, changes, risks, evidence, and debates.

The book builds four layers:

1. Domain Understanding（领域理解）
2. Domain Language（领域语言）
3. Reading Comprehension（阅读理解）
4. Test Application（考试应用）

The main body focuses on the first two layers. IELTS question-solving belongs to the application layer and can be developed later as exercises, a workbook, or a second book.

---

# 2. Core Writing Style（核心写作风格）

## 2.1 English Main Text（英文正文为主）

Each chapter is written mainly in English.

Chinese appears only inside brackets after important concepts, mechanisms, or terms.

Example:

> A forest is a functional ecological system（功能性生态系统）that holds soil, regulates water, stores carbon, shelters species, and supports the continuous movement of energy and nutrients.

---

## 2.2 Chinese Bracket Explanation（中文括号解释）

Chinese explanations are used to support understanding, not to replace English reading.

Use this format:

> resilience（韧性 / 恢复能力）
> habitat fragmentation（栖息地破碎化）
> nutrient cycling（养分循环）
> food security（粮食安全 / 稳定获得足够食物的能力）

For difficult concepts, the Chinese explanation can be slightly longer:

> risk factor（提高某种结果发生概率的因素）
> ecosystem resilience（生态系统承受扰动并继续运行的能力）

---

## 2.3 Affirmative Exposition（肯定式表达）

The writing directly establishes correct understanding.

Preferred style:

> A forest is a functional ecological system.
> Biodiversity expresses the richness of life inside an ecosystem.
> Conservation focuses on maintaining ecological functions and preventing further loss.

Less preferred style:

> A forest is not just a group of trees.
> Biodiversity is not simply the number of species.
> Conservation is not merely protection.

The book speaks like a domain expert explaining the world, not like a teacher correcting misunderstandings.

---

## 2.4 Content Before Form（内容先于形式）

Each section begins directly with domain content.

Preferred opening:

> Soil stores water, supports plant roots, holds nutrients, and provides living space for microorganisms. Its structure determines how easily water enters the ground and how well plants recover after disturbance.

Less preferred opening:

> Many students think soil is simple, but this understanding is too shallow.

The writing should enter the thing itself: forests, soil, water, cities, markets, memory, public health, technology, evidence, and culture.

---

## 2.5 Mechanism-Based Writing（机制化写作）

Each section explains how things work.

Every important concept should be connected to:

* structure（结构）
* function（功能）
* process（过程）
* cause（原因）
* effect（影响）
* interaction（相互作用）
* change（变化）
* risk（风险）
* evidence（证据）
* limitation（限制）

Example:

> Automation（自动化）changes work by transferring repetitive tasks from humans to machines. This raises productivity（生产率）, reduces labour costs（劳动力成本）, changes skill demand（技能需求）, and reshapes the labour market（劳动市场）.

# 3. Chapter Writing Structure（每章写作结构）

Each chapter contains 5–8 sections.

Each section should be 1500–3000 English words in the full book version. For drafting, one section can be written at a time.

Each section follows this internal rhythm:

## 3.1 Domain Opening（领域开场）

Start with a concrete domain statement.

Example:

> A city is a spatial system（空间系统）that concentrates people, housing, transport, services, work, consumption, and public infrastructure.

## 3.2 Mechanism Development（机制展开）

Explain how the domain works.

Example:

> As population density（人口密度）increases, demand for housing rises. Higher land demand can raise living costs, push residents farther from employment centres, increase commuting time, and place pressure on public transport.

## 3.3 Domain Language Integration（领域语言嵌入）

Introduce English terms naturally inside the mechanism.

Example:

> commuting（通勤）connects housing location with work opportunity. Long commuting time reduces personal time, increases transport demand, and can lower quality of life（生活质量）.

## 3.4 Conceptual Deepening（概念深化）

Explain important concepts beyond dictionary meaning.

Example:

> infrastructure（基础设施）refers to the systems that allow a city to function: roads, railways, water supply, electricity, waste treatment, communication networks, hospitals, schools, and public services.

## 3.5 Section Closure（小节收束）

End by summarising the domain logic.

Example:

> Urbanisation（城市化）therefore describes more than population movement. It describes the concentration of people, services, resources, risks, and opportunities inside limited space.

# 4. Final Book Structure（最终全书结构）

## Part I: Topic Worlds and Academic Reading

## 第一部分：主题世界与学术阅读

### Chapter 1: Topic Worlds and Domain Language（主题世界与领域语言）

This chapter introduces the method of the book. IELTS Reading texts often explain phenomena, mechanisms, evidence, change, risk, and debate inside specific topic worlds. Reading becomes easier when the reader understands how a domain organises reality and how English expresses that organisation.

Sections:

1.1 Topic Worlds in IELTS Reading（雅思阅读中的主题世界）
1.2 Domain Language as a Way of Understanding（作为理解方式的领域语言）
1.3 From Words to Systems（从单词到系统）
1.4 From Domain Understanding to Reading Comprehension（从领域理解到阅读理解）
1.5 Test Application as a Later Layer（作为后续层的考试应用）

Writing focus:

This chapter establishes the core principle:

> Reading grows from understanding how a domain talks about the world.

# 5. Final Chapter Production Rules（最终章节生产规则）

## Rule 1: Write the main text in English.

The reader should spend most of the time reading English.

## Rule 2: Add Chinese only for key concepts.

Use Chinese brackets to explain important terms, mechanisms, and abstract ideas.

## Rule 3: Use affirmative domain exposition.

Build the correct view directly.

## Rule 4: Start from content.

Begin with the domain object, process, or mechanism.

## Rule 5: Develop one mechanism per section.

Each section should focus on one coherent mechanism.

## Rule 6: Let vocabulary grow from the domain.

English expressions should appear because the domain needs them.

## Rule 7: Keep IELTS as the boundary.

The depth should match educated general reading and IELTS-style academic texts.

## Rule 8: Place test practice after domain language.

Question-solving can be developed later as an application layer.
